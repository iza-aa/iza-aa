Software engineer at **Badan Sistem Informasi (BSI)**, based in Indonesia.

---

### Featured Work & Impact

- **[iza-sppg-agent](https://github.com/iza-aa/iza-sppg-agent)** — Multimodal AI bot for school nutrition catering (MBG). Extracts physical market receipts using Gemini Vision, validates government budget ceilings, and syncs directly to Google Sheets (213 automated tests).
- **[iza-wa-agent](https://github.com/iza-aa/iza-wa-agent)** — Autonomous WhatsApp commerce assistant for MSMEs. Parses voice notes, receipt photos, and informal text into accounting ledgers with zero JSON leak and cashier handover (248 commits).
- **[iza-pos-v2](https://github.com/iza-aa/iza-pos-v2)** — Cloud restaurant POS and store operations system. Handles self-service QR table ordering, live kitchen queues, and tamper-resistant geofenced staff attendance kiosks ([Live Demo](https://iza-pos-v2.vercel.app)).
- **[izanotch](https://github.com/iza-aa/izanotch)** — Local-first AI desktop companion for macOS. Sits in the MacBook notch display geometry to automate computer-use workflows via Swift, Rust Tauri, and Model Context Protocol (MCP).
- **[bsirelay](https://github.com/iza-aa/bsirelay)** — Zero-dependency MCP relay and CLI toolkit standardizing guidelines and memory for 15+ AI coding tools across enterprise teams.
- **[hidrolux](https://github.com/iza-aa/hidrolux)** — Corporate engineering showcase platform for an industrial water treatment equipment firm ([Live Demo](https://hidro-phi.vercel.app)).
